## Bloopi plush line

The current species sheet describes Bloopis as small, fuzzy, light-blue floaters with huge eyes and rounded gelatinous appendages. It defines five in-world size ranges: Tiny at 5 to 7 cm, Small at 10 to 12 cm, Medium at 18 to 22 cm, Large at 28 to 32 cm, and Giant at 40 to 45 cm. The species has a white-fur form as well.

The Bible includes Bloopi creature art and those size variations, but I found no earlier plush concept or merchandise specification in the project files I reviewed. This new concept proposes carrying the five in-world size classes into a plush range, with a white companion edition as a character variant.

These are proposed toy dimensions based on the creature's established in-world scales. Final sizes should be confirmed through sample construction and safety review.

## Product recognition cues

- Keep the round, fuzzy silhouette and very large expressive eyes across every size.
- Use soft, rounded appendages that read as flexible gel forms in the game and safe sewn features in the toy.
- Keep blue as the base color family. Treat the white form as a distinct character edition.
- Vary facial expression and pose while retaining a consistent body shape and recognizable face.
