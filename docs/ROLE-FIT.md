I reported approximately $20K per month in reduced external development expense after delivery moved in-house. The broader profile describes supporting evidence and limits.

The portfolio materials reviewed do not establish shipped commercial game credits, a level-blockout portfolio, live-game economy tuning, or experience leading a design team. I am targeting associate and hands-on designer roles where a strong systems portfolio and transferable implementation experience can be evaluated directly.

- What is a design target, what has implementation evidence, and what still needs testing.
